# Character parallels

## Confirmed direction: Shen / Shane Battier

Shen is King Poon's adviser, inspired by his former teammate Shane Battier. In Chapter 1, Poon recognizes something familiar when Shen approaches him and asks whether he is Shane. Shen introduces himself and does not recognize the name Battier. Keep the resemblance suggestive; whether these are counterparts across universes remains unresolved.

Write Shen as observant, strategically capable, and quietly funny. He should contribute judgments Poon respects, with the confidence to disagree. His attention goes to preparation, defensive positions, and small decisions that make a larger plan work. Give him interests and responsibilities beyond explaining the kingdom or carrying paperwork.

Potential development, not yet established in the story: Shen was a respected tournament specialist before becoming an adviser. His competitive past and reasons for stepping away could emerge later.

## Possible future characters — not yet canon

### Dwyane Wade: the trusted fellow champion

A celebrated player from a southern kingdom who welcomes Poon as an equal. Their friendship could grow through shared games, teasing, and mutual respect. He has his own achievements and obligations, and can challenge Poon without courtly ceremony. Best suited to companionship and emotional warmth. Name and introduction remain undecided.

### Udonis Haslem: the veteran captain

A longstanding leader of the public game halls, closely connected to their players and neighborhoods. Blunt, loyal, and protective of standards, he holds Poon accountable for the promises that opened those halls. His authority comes from years of service. Name and introduction remain undecided.

### Rajon Rondo: the former rival and tactician

A brilliant opposing player who might eventually join Poon's circle. He remembers past positions, questions assumptions, and spots traps early. His willingness to argue can produce both useful insights and friction. Give him his own ambitions and reasons to cooperate. Name and introduction remain undecided.

### Kyrie Irving: the inventive challenger

A gifted player whose fluid, surprising combinations make even familiar games feel unpredictable. Poon recognizes the pleasure of playing alongside someone who can improvise under pressure, but this character also wants room to pursue their own ideas and reputation. They could first meet as opponents at a public game hall, then discover how well their different instincts work together in a paired tournament. Keep the focus on creativity, competitive trust, and independence. Name, role, and introduction remain undecided.

## Planned future encounter: the Chef / Steph Curry

King Poon will later meet the head court cook, a fictional parallel to Steph Curry built around the “Chef Curry” nickname. The encounter is approved as a future story direction; its chapter placement and the cook's personal name remain undecided.

The court knows this character simply as the Chef. Give him quick hands, an easy manner, and exacting standards beneath his apparent effortlessness. Cooking is a real craft and responsibility for him, with a kitchen staff, demanding banquets, and a life beyond Poon's recognition.

Possible introduction: Poon follows an enticing smell into the palace kitchens after a late game. The Chef sends a pinch of seasoning into a distant pot without interrupting his conversation. Poon stops in the doorway, struck by the familiar motion and face. Their exchange can hint at recognition without repeating Shen's introduction word for word.

Potential recurring role: the kitchen becomes an informal place for Poon to unwind, exchange news, and discover that the Chef is a formidable board-game opponent. His style could make neglected corners of the board dangerous, echoing the threat of scoring from distance. Treat him as a familiar competitive counterpart, rather than assuming every parallel was Poon's former club teammate.

## Writing approach

These are fictional characters inspired by basketball relationships and playing roles. Develop their lives within the Poon Kingdom. Introduce parallels gradually as the plot needs them. Shen's parallel is established in Chapter 1; meeting the Chef is planned for a later chapter. Wade, Haslem, Rondo, and Kyrie remain potential additions.
