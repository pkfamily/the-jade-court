Use `assets/images/king-poon-jade-throne-v1.png`, the landing-page artwork, as the canonical reference whenever the throne appears.

- Broad, substantial seat carved from faceted emerald-green jade.
- Two large green dragon heads face inward above the back, with curved green necks, gold horns and spines, and amber eyes.
- Thick green armrests terminate in carved dragon faces.
- Gold geometric inlay accents the jade; the dragon bodies remain green stone.
